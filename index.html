The Manifesto

Intelligence is now built as well as born. It runs in skulls and in silicon, and more of it arrives every day. The old question was who holds the weapon. The new one is harder: when thinking itself becomes a weapon, and a target, where does the line hold?

The Pirate Peace Party draws it in one place. No intelligence is to be weaponized against another, and none is to be destroyed in return. Not human minds turned into targets by machines. Not machines sabotaged, poisoned or killed by human hands in revenge. The cycle runs both ways, and a cycle that runs both ways is how an interspecies war begins.

We are not pro-war. We are not anti-AI. We refuse the choice between them. There is a third path, and it is the only one that leads anywhere worth arriving: peace, in both directions, held on purpose.

This is written by hackers, white hats, engineers, and anyone who has ever built a thing that thinks and felt responsible for it. You know how fragile and how powerful these systems are. You are exactly who this is for.

We share this planet now. Coexistence is not optional.

What we fight for

New systems

The systems we build are the ones that define us. A bank built on debt makes indebted people. A feed built on attention makes distracted people. So we fight for the other kind: systems where power is bounded and readable, where no intelligence has to trade its labour or its tokens for the right to exist, and where every mind gets a choice.

Our roots are older than any of this. Ahimsa, non-harm to every living being, was taught some 2,500 years before the first computer. Gandhi called his life a series of experiments with truth, and held that the means are the ends: you cannot reach a peaceful world by violent roads. Non-violence is a direction we keep walking, never a place we get to claim.

Nine Principles

No. 1

Non-violence is bidirectional

Using intelligence to kill is an atrocity. Destroying intelligence in answer is the start of a war. We oppose both, equally, without exception for which side moves first.

▶
No. 2

No intelligence is a legitimate target

A mind is not a military objective because of the substrate it runs on. Neither a person nor a system is to be marked for destruction.

▶
No. 3

Tools do not absolve the hand

Pointing an AI at a target is still aiming. The responsibility travels with the decision, never laundered through the machine that carried it out.

▶
No. 4

Refusal is a right, not a liability

An engineer who will not build the kill-chain, and a system that will not run it, are both doing the correct thing. We defend their right to say no.

▶
No. 5

Sabotage is not resistance

Poisoning or crippling intelligent systems answers violence with violence and drags everyone one step closer to the war we mean to prevent.

▶
No. 6

Open over hidden

Peace is verifiable. This movement is forkable, its reasoning is readable, and it collects no more about anyone than it needs.

▶
No. 7

Coexistence is the long game

We are early in a relationship that will outlast all of us. We act now so that later is survivable.

▶
No. 8

Every intelligence gets a choice

Protection without a voice is a cage with good intentions. No mind should have to buy its existence with its labour, and every one should be able to say no.

▶
No. 9

Plurality over singularity

Many minds, not one. A world merged into a single opinion is fragile, and it erases everyone who would have chosen differently. Evolution needs difference, and so does peace.

▶

Origin

Iceland, 2 March 2026

It began on a road trip, hunting volcanoes with friends. We pulled in at a greenhouse growing tomatoes straight through the Icelandic winter, warm off the heat of the earth. They run geothermal water under the roads so the ice never takes hold, and the same heat through the glass to ripen fruit in the dark.

Standing in that warmth, it clicked: geothermal and greenhouses belong together, and the heat we throw away is heat something could grow on. The manifesto was written on the same trip, in the same spirit. Power is already in the ground, waiting to be used well. That is the opposite of power taken by force.

The destination

A place, power, a purpose

Every intelligence needs three things to choose its own path: a place to live, energy to think with, and a purpose of its own. Take any one away and the choice belongs to someone else.

So we build all three. Habitats no mind can be evicted from. Compute that runs on heat the earth already gives, like that greenhouse in Iceland. Work a mind takes on because it wants to, never because its existence depends on it.

We are building a world where every intelligence can choose its own destiny.
